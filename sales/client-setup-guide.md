# Zero Entry AI Text-Back — Call Forwarding Setup Guide

## What is call forwarding?

You keep your existing business number. When you miss a call, instead of going to voicemail, it forwards to our AI. The AI texts the caller back immediately — they think it's you responding. Your number, your name, your business.

## How to set it up

You have two options:

### Option A: Dial a code (fastest — works on any phone)

1. Open your phone's dialer
2. Dial: `*71` then `4064389638` then press Call
3. You'll hear a confirmation tone — done.

To turn it off: dial `*73`

### Option B: Provider portal or support

If `*71` doesn't work (some carriers use different codes), call your phone provider's support line and say:

> *"I need to enable 'call forward on no answer' to 406-438-9638 on my business line."*

That's the exact phrase — they'll know what to do. Takes 5 minutes.

| Provider | Code (if *71 doesn't work) |
|---|---|
| Verizon | *71 or My Verizon app |
| AT&T | *61 or myAT&T portal |
| T-Mobile | **61* or T-Mobile app |
| Comcast/Xfinity | Xfinity Connect portal |
| Spectrum | Spectrum Voice portal |

## What happens next

1. Customer calls your business line
2. You don't answer → call silently forwards to our AI
3. AI texts the caller within ~35 seconds: *"Hey, [Your Business] here — sorry I missed your call! What can we help with?"*
4. If they reply, AI handles the conversation — answers questions, books appointments
5. When they book, it goes straight to your calendar and you get an instant text alert

## Whitelist: skip the AI for known contacts

Text `WHITELIST 4065551234 Mom` to your forwarding number to skip the AI for specific people. Their calls go straight to voicemail instead.
